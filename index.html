Who I am

Where disciplined thinking meets practical clarity

I work with founders and executives at the stages where positioning, capital, government relationships, market strategy and communications all have to move together — a fundraising round, a market entry, a high-stakes stakeholder conversation.

Most of the teams I meet have technology that works. What stalls the round, the introduction, or the procurement conversation is usually somewhere else — the story hasn't been built for the institution on the other side of the table.

How I work

A focused, one-person practice by design. You work directly with me — no account manager, no junior analyst relaying between you and the person making the call.

When specialist execution is needed — design, media buying, event production — I work with your existing providers or a recommended partner. I advise and shape strategy; I don't run campaigns.

Who I work with

Defence and dual-use companies

My primary clients are founders, CEOs and leadership teams building or scaling technologies relevant to defence, security and resilience. I also work selectively with investors, industry organisations and public-sector initiatives where my experience is relevant.

About

Experience built where strategy meets defence reality

Serhii Nabok
Principal Advisor — Strategic Communications & Defence Narrative

I lead Bohun Defence Advisory as a strategic communications and policy professional with more than 15 years of experience across government, defence, international organisations, media and business. Based in Lisbon, I work primarily across Ukraine and Europe.
