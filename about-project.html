How to interpret the data

Public beta. Coverage is partial and changing. Information may be incomplete, outdated or revised. Inclusion is not independent verification or endorsement. Consult original sources before relying on a claim.

Map entries are not a list of BOHUN clients, customers or partners. City-centre markers are not facility addresses. The map does not establish territorial status.

Machine-prepared syntheses and original publisher reporting are distinct. An editorial approval label is not a substitute for checking cited evidence.

Research principles

  1. Keep source reporting distinguishable from interpretation.
  2. Show the limits of public-data coverage.
  3. Do not imply business relationships from inclusion in the index.
  4. Revise records when stronger evidence becomes available.
