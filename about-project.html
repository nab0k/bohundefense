About BOHUN Defence Intelligence

BOHUN combines defence and dual-use market intelligence with hands-on business development. The platform is built for founders, industry professionals, investors and consultants who want to connect individual developments with the wider market.

Read the market in context

Explore source-linked syntheses alongside the company directory and interactive map. Syntheses bring related developments and background together; sources and dates help readers trace the reporting.

How materials are prepared

Automated tools assist with collecting, assembling and checking material. Our approach distinguishes announcements from agreements, investment plans from completed capacity, and company statements from independent corroboration. The method is being tested and refined as coverage grows.

We are developing a separate signals format to explain a documented change, why it matters and what evidence would strengthen or overturn the interpretation.

Sources and coverage

The platform uses publicly available information. Coverage and company profiles are partial and evolving. Inclusion is not an endorsement or evidence of a client relationship; original sources remain available for readers to assess.
