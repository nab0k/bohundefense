About the project

A defence market intelligence project, growing every day

BOHUN follows the defence and dual-use industry through open sources and connects individual developments with the companies, programmes and places behind them. The platform is in active development: companies, news and analysis are added continuously.

What you can use today

  • Today: source-linked syntheses that put a new development in context.
  • Atlas: an interactive map of companies and events.
  • Company directory: a searchable research index.

What is being added

  • New sources and companies, as they are found and checked.
  • Signals: a separate format that explains a documented market change and the evidence behind it.
  • Analysis that goes deeper into selected developments.

How materials are prepared

Automated tools assist with collecting, assembling and checking material. Our approach distinguishes announcements from agreements, investment plans from completed capacity, and company statements from independent corroboration. The method is being tested and refined as coverage grows.

Sources and coverage

The platform uses publicly available information. Coverage and company profiles are partial and evolving. Most companies are unverified research candidates. Inclusion is not an endorsement or evidence of a client relationship; original sources remain available for readers to assess. City-centre markers are not facility addresses.
