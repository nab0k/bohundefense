Services

Move a specific commercial opportunity forward. BOHUN works with defence and dual-use companies on customers, suppliers, partnerships and private investment discussions.

Buyers and new business

For companies with a product seeking customers and commercial partners. Work can include market research, positioning, targeted introductions and support through negotiations.

Suppliers and qualification

For buyers seeking a manufacturer or components that meet their requirements. Work can include identifying candidates, screening available evidence against the agreed requirements, and supporting discussions to establish a workable supply relationship.

Technology partnerships

For teams seeking testing, integration or commercial partners. We identify relevant organisations and help shape and advance a specific collaboration.

Private investment opportunities

For owners and investors exploring a stake sale or acquisition. Opportunities are discussed discreetly with selected counterparties; scope and disclosure are agreed in advance.

Working together

Each engagement starts with a concrete objective, an agreed scope and remuneration. Success-based arrangements may be appropriate.
