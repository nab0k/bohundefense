Privacy notice

Information collected

Forms may collect your name, organisation, work email, the topic and what you describe, and details of a company or source you submit. It is used to review and respond to enquiries, assess potential professional engagement and check company data.

Form processing

Public forms are processed by Formspree under its terms. You may instead contact nabok@bohundefence.com.

Retention, sharing and requests

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to respond, manage potential engagements or comply with applicable law; it is not sold. Data may be shared with the providers operating email and forms or when required by law. For access, corrections or deletion requests, email the address above.
