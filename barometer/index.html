How this could play out

This gap isn't a new phenomenon — it's a documented one. U.S. defence-acquisition oversight (GAO) has flagged for years that contractors tend to be more optimistic about technology readiness than independent assessment shows, which is exactly why independent readiness reviews are required before contract award. Below is one illustrative case showing what it looks like to catch that same kind of gap earlier — not five separate illustrative numbers, but one thread through all five layers of the console.

Illustrative · Autonomous Logistics, Series A

Technology

An autonomous ground-resupply platform, proven in GNSS-denied combat conditions — the kind of validation that earns an investor's first call.

Perception gap

Zooming out to the whole Autonomous Logistics theme, the Perception Gap tab shows a 26-point split: manufacturers rate technology readiness at 75, investors at 49 — the widest gap tracked this wave. This is a market-level read, not a score for this specific company.

4
Accessibility

And market access can complicate the picture further: checking the target export market shows real budget alongside high local-partnership and regulatory friction — big, not yet easy to enter.

5
Investment implication

This doesn't tell you whether the company is ready. It tells you what to verify. The theme-level disagreement flags technical readiness as a specific assumption worth testing in this company's own due diligence — not a verdict on the company itself.
